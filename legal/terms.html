Purpose and operation

The app helps transfer text and links between accessible applications. Features depend on macOS permissions and the accessibility support of the source and destination apps. Some editors, documents, or tables may not be supported. Beta functions, including multiselect, may change or behave unexpectedly.

Check the destination and inserted content. A table-cell drop may replace the cell’s value. The clipboard may retain transferred text if insertion cannot be confirmed. Save important work and use the destination app’s undo facilities where appropriate.
